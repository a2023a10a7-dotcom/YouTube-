# Sources — The Great Molasses Flood of 1919

## Primary and institutional sources

- [City of Boston — 100 years ago today: Molasses crashes through Boston's North End](https://www.boston.gov/news/100-years-ago-today-molasses-crashes-through-bostons-north-end) — municipal historical account; tank, location, 2.3 million gallons, damage, response and casualty figures.
- [Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth — The Boston Molasses Flood](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/state-house-tours/did-you-know/Molasses-Flood.htm) — state historical account; final toll, cleanup, litigation and inquiry.
- [Commonwealth of Massachusetts — historical account of the Molasses Flood](https://archives.lib.state.ma.us/server/api/core/bitstreams/041fa4e1-3de8-44f8-9fa6-490007ade732/content) — state archive publication with historical context.

## Reporting and explanatory context

- [HISTORY — Why the Great Molasses Flood Was So Deadly](https://www.history.com/articles/great-molasses-flood-science) — engineering and scientific context; use as secondary explanation.
- [WBUR — Remembering Boston's Deadly Molasses Flood, 100 Years Later](https://www.wbur.org/news/2019/01/15/boston-molasses-flood-100-years-later) — centennial reporting and local recollections.
- [HISTORY — The Great Molasses Flood of 1919](https://www.history.com/articles/the-great-molasses-flood-of-1919) — secondary narrative overview.

## Editorial caveats

- Sources differ on the tank's capacity and the wave's height/speed. The narration uses the City of Boston's 2.3-million-gallon figure and avoids precise wave measurements.
- Archival image rights must be checked individually before publication. A historical photograph being online does not automatically make it public domain.
- Any recreated diagram or map must be labeled as a reconstruction.
